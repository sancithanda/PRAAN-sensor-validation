# Final Sensor Placement

The experimental placement study resulted in the following
proposed sensor locations:

- **MPU6050 / BMA400 → Waist / lower torso**
  - Selected for stronger impact separation and lower
    motion artefacts.

- **MAX30102 → Left chest**
  - Selected for stronger PPG signal and comparatively
    lower motion artefacts.

- **Chest Sound Sensor → Upper chest / sternum**
  - Selected for stronger respiratory waveform and higher SNR.

These positions are incorporated into the proposed PRAAN jacket
architecture.
